\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a zone within the event. 

\doxylink{class_event_zone}{Event\+Zone} acts as a \doxylink{class_subject}{Subject} in the \doxylink{class_observer}{Observer} pattern, allowing observers such as \doxylink{class_student_guide}{Student\+Guide} to be attached and moved between zones at runtime.
