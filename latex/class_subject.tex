\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{class_subject}{Subject} interface used by the Event\+Flow \doxylink{class_observer}{Observer} pattern. 

A \doxylink{class_subject}{Subject} maintains a collection of Observers and notifies them when an \doxylink{class_event_notice}{Event\+Notice} is generated.
